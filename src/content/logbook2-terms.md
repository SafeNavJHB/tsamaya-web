## 2. Important notice about regulations

Logbook² produces logbook pages and summaries formatted to the layout used under SA-CATS 61 (the technical standards to Part 61 of the South African Civil Aviation Regulations).

**Logbook² is not approved, endorsed or certified by the South African Civil Aviation Authority (SACAA). No such approval scheme exists for electronic logbooks.**

You, the pilot, remain solely responsible for:

- the accuracy, completeness and legal validity of your logbook;

- printing and filing your pages at least every 90 days, as that regulation requires;
- keeping any signatures and certifications you need;
- retaining your records for the required period.

Totals, recency and currency figures shown by the app are calculated from what you entered. They are aids only. They are not a determination of legal currency, and you must not rely on them as one.
