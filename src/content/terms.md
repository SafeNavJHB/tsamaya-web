## 1. What Tsamaya is, and what it is not

Tsamaya is an **information tool**. It suggests driving routes with lower statistical exposure to areas and roads showing elevated risk in **historical, statistical data** (public crime statistics, mapping data, and curated review).

Tsamaya is **not** a safety guarantee, a security service, an emergency service, or a substitute for your own judgement.

## 2. The risk disclaimer (read this one)

- **"Lower-risk" means lower statistical exposure relative to a default route. It does not mean "safe." No route anywhere is free of risk.**
- Our data is historical and periodically updated. Conditions change faster than any dataset: incidents occur on roads our data rates well, and routes our data rates poorly may be uneventful.
- Coverage is limited to specific South African metros and is not exhaustive within them. Absence of a marked zone does not mean absence of risk.
- Routing depends on third-party services (mapping, traffic) and device sensors (GPS) that can be wrong, stale, or unavailable.

Every feature that routes you around risk (all route options, risk alerts, Guardian, reports, CarPlay and Android Auto) stays free and never depends on Premium.

- **Renewal and cancelling.** Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each period. - **Estimates.** Arrive by works out a time to leave from typical traffic for that weekday and time, and fuel cost multiplies a route's length by the fuel use you set and a price a litre: yours if you set one, otherwise the official price for the area the trip starts in, or ends in when it starts outside the areas Tsamaya covers (for diesel, the official wholesale price, which is below what pumps charge). They are used once that device's daily share is used up, or while the free detailed searches Tsamaya shares among free devices each day have run out, one for each detailed search that Google answers (a search that fails is not counted), and they do not expire. When Google's daily limit for all of Tsamaya, or the daily allowance for pack searches across Tsamaya, is reached, search uses the free alternatives for the rest of that day, and your pack searches wait for you.
