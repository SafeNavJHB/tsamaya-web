> 4.1 This clause is a notice in terms of section 49 (Notice required for certain terms and conditions) of the Consumer Protection Act. Please read it carefully: it is shown to you in plain language, and you must acknowledge it, before the App plans your first route (Appendix A).

> 4.2 **"Lower-risk" means lower statistical exposure relative to a default route. It does not mean "safe." No route anywhere is free of risk.**

> 4.3 Our data is historical and is updated periodically. Conditions change faster than any dataset: incidents occur on roads our data rates well, and routes our data rates poorly may be uneventful.

> 4.5 Risk ratings describe places, not people. They are not statements of fact about, and do not refer to or reflect on, any person, business, property owner, community or resident in or near a road or area. A report to us is not a report to the police: please report any crime to the SAPS.

## 7. Sharing your Trip, SOS and Guardian

> 7.1 Live trip sharing, the SOS alert and Guardian let someone you choose follow your drive on a link. They depend on your phone's location, its internet connection and battery, on our servers, and on the person you chose looking at the link and acting on it. Updates can be late, inaccurate or interrupted. The SOS alert opens a message to your emergency contact, which you send yourself; it does not contact the police, an ambulance or a security company. Anyone who has a link can see the trip while it is shared, so send it only to people you trust. **In an emergency, call 10111 or 112.**
