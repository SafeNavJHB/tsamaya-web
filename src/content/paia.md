# Tsamaya PAIA Manual

**Prepared in terms of section 51 of the Promotion of Access to Information Act 2 of 2000 (PAIA), as amended, and the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA). Date of compilation: 12 October 2026. Effective date: 1 November 2026.**

## 1. Introduction

1.1 The Promotion of Access to Information Act 2 of 2000 gives effect to the right of access to information in section 32 of the Constitution of the Republic of South Africa, 1996. It gives you the right to request access to any Record held by a private body, such as Tsamaya, where that Record is required for the exercise or protection of any of your rights, as provided in section 50 (Right of access to records of private bodies) of PAIA.

1.2 This PAIA Manual is published in terms of section 51 (Manual) of PAIA and, as far as it describes how we Process Personal Information, section 17 (Documentation) of POPIA. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy, which explains what Personal Information we collect and why and how a Data Subject may exercise the rights described in clause 17 of the Privacy Policy, and our Terms of Use.

1.3 This PAIA Manual is available free of charge on our Website and for inspection at our address, as described in clause 12.

## 5. Which Procedure Should I Use?

5.1 You have rights under both POPIA and PAIA. Choosing the right procedure will help us to deal with your request quickly and, in most cases, free of charge. The table below explains which procedure to use:

| What do you want to do? | Procedure to use |
|---|---|
| Find out whether we hold Personal Information about you. | Clause 18 of the Privacy Policy and the Data Subject Request Form (Annexure B), free of charge (section 23(1)(a) (Access to personal information) of POPIA). |
| Obtain a copy or description of your own Personal Information, and the identity of the persons who have had access to it. | Clause 18 of the Privacy Policy and the Data Subject Request Form (Annexure B) (section 23(1)(b) (Access to personal information) of POPIA). Because section 25 (Manner of access) of POPIA applies section 53 of PAIA, a request for copies of Records is dealt with as set out in clause 11, without a request fee. |
| Correct, delete or destroy your Personal Information. | Clause 18 of the Privacy Policy, using the Data Subject Request Form (Annexure B) (section 24 (Correction of personal information) of POPIA). |
| Object to the Processing of your Personal Information or to direct marketing, or withdraw your consent. | Clause 18 of the Privacy Policy, using the Data Subject Request Form (Annexure B) (section 11(3) (Consent, justification and objection) of POPIA). |
| Obtain access to any other Record held by Tsamaya that you need to exercise or protect a right. | This PAIA Manual and the PAIA Request Form (Annexure A) (section 50 (Right of access to records of private bodies) of PAIA). |
| Complain about our decision or about how we handle your Personal Information. | Clause 11.6 of this PAIA Manual, clause 18 of the Privacy Policy and the Information Regulator. |

5.2 You do not need to use the PAIA procedure to find out whether we hold your Personal Information, to correct or delete it, to object to its Processing or to withdraw your consent; these requests are dealt with free of charge under clause 18 of the Privacy Policy. If you are unsure which procedure to use, you may use either form or simply contact our Information Officer. We will not refuse to consider a request merely because the wrong form was used.

5.3 PAIA does not apply to a Record requested for the purpose of criminal or civil proceedings after those proceedings have commenced, where the production of or access to that Record is provided for in any other law, as provided in section 7 (Act not applying to records requested for criminal or civil proceedings after commencement) of PAIA. In that case, the Record must be requested under the rules of the relevant court or tribunal.

10.4.1 Our service providers Process Personal Information outside South Africa: in the United Kingdom, the European Union and the United States. We transfer it only as section 72 (Transfers of personal information outside Republic) of POPIA allows: the Recipient is bound by law or by binding data processing terms that give protection substantially similar to POPIA, and the transfer is necessary to provide the service the person asked for. ### 10.5 Security measures

10.5.1 Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) on every network call.

10.5.2 Row-level security on our database; reports can be added by the App but not read back with its public key, and only authorised reviewers can read them.

10.5.3 Data minimisation: saved places, trip history and the logbook stay on the driver's phone; network addresses with police notices are stored only as a keyed hash.

10.5.4 A retention schedule enforced by automatic daily deletion jobs.

10.5.5 Data Processing terms with every service provider.

10.5.6 A security compromise is reported to the Information Regulator, through its eServices portal, and to the people affected, as section 22 (Notification of security compromises) of POPIA requires.

### 10.6 Retention

10.6.1 We keep Personal Information only for as long as we need it for the purpose for which we collected it, unless a longer period is required or authorised by law, as section 14 (Retention and restriction of records) of POPIA permits. The specific maximum period for each category of Personal Information is set out in clause 15 of our Privacy Policy. When a period ends, we delete or destroy the Personal Information, or de-identify it so that it can no longer be linked to the Data Subject.

## 11. How to Request Access to a Record

### 11.1 The request

11.1.1 Complete the PAIA Request Form, available on the Information Regulator's website and attached to this manual as Annexure A, and email it to our Information Officer at info@tsamayaapp.co.za. Give enough detail for us to identify the Record and you, say in which form you want access, and identify the right you want to exercise or protect and explain why the Record is needed for it (section 53 (Form of request) of PAIA). If you ask on behalf of someone else, include proof of your authority to do so. Attach proof of your identity; for a request about the information linked to your installation, the Installation ID described in clause 11.2 is enough, and we will not ask for an identity document.

11.1.2 If you cannot read or write, or have a disability that prevents you from completing the PAIA Request Form, you may make your request orally to our Information Officer, who will complete the form for you and give you a copy.

11.1.3 We will acknowledge receipt of your request in writing and give you a reference number, which you should quote in all correspondence about your request.

### 11.2 Your own Personal Information

11.2.1 If you want to know what Personal Information we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted, you may email us (sections 23 (Access to personal information) and 24 (Correction of personal information) of POPIA), or use the Data Subject Request Form (Annexure B), which also covers objections to our Processing (section 11(3) (Consent, justification and objection) of POPIA) and requests for correction or deletion (section 24 (Correction of personal information) of POPIA). Tsamaya has no accounts, so include the **Installation ID** from the App (Settings › Help & About), or tell us what you sent and roughly when. We do not charge for confirming what we hold about you or for an electronic copy of it. Clause 18 of our Privacy Policy explains how we deal with these requests, including verification, timelines and fees.

### 11.3 Fees

11.3.1 The fees are those prescribed by the PAIA Regulations:

11.3.1.1 a request fee of R140.00, which a Personal Requester (someone asking for their own Personal Information) does not pay;

11.3.1.2 R2.00 for each printed A4 page;

11.3.1.3 R40.00 for a copy on a flash drive you provide;

11.3.1.4 R145.00 for each hour, after the first, needed to search for and prepare a Record, up to R435.00;

11.3.1.5 a deposit of one third of the access fee when the search will take more than six hours;

11.3.1.6 postage at actual cost.

11.3.2 Most of our Records can be sent by email, and we will tell you before any fee is due.

11.3.3 If your request is refused, we will refund any deposit you have paid. Each notice requiring you to pay a fee or a deposit will tell you that you may complain to the Information Regulator, or apply to court, about the fee or the deposit.

### 11.4 Time to decide

11.4.1 We decide on a request within 30 days of receiving it in full. We may extend that once, by up to 30 days, in the circumstances PAIA allows, and we will tell you why. If a Record contains information about a Third Party, we must, within 21 days of receiving the request, take reasonable steps to tell that Third Party about the request and give it an opportunity to respond (section 71 (Notice to third parties) of PAIA). The Third Party may, within 21 days of being told, make representations to us or consent to the disclosure, and we will decide the request within 30 days after telling the Third Party. If we do not decide within the time PAIA allows, the request is regarded as refused (section 58 (Deemed refusal of request) of PAIA).

11.4.2 If we grant your request, we will give access in the form you asked for, unless this would interfere unreasonably with our operations, damage the Record or infringe copyright not owned by us, in which case we may give access in another form (section 60 (Form of access) of PAIA).

11.4.3 If, after taking all reasonable steps, we cannot find a Record or believe that it does not exist, we will tell you by affidavit or affirmation, giving details of the steps taken to find it (section 55 (Records that cannot be found or do not exist) of PAIA). This notice is treated as a refusal of your request. If the Record is found later, we will give you access to it unless a ground of refusal applies.

### 11.5 Grounds for refusal

11.5.1 We may or must refuse access on the grounds in Chapter 4 of Part 3 of PAIA (sections 63 to 70), including to protect: the privacy of a third person; a Third Party's commercial information or confidential information; the safety of individuals and property; Records privileged from production in legal proceedings; our own commercial information; and research information.

11.5.2 Despite these grounds, we must give access to a Record if its disclosure would reveal evidence of a substantial contravention of, or failure to comply with, the law, or an imminent and serious public safety or environmental risk, and the public interest in disclosure clearly outweighs the harm contemplated by the ground of refusal (section 70 (Mandatory disclosure in public interest) of PAIA). Where only part of a Record may be refused, we will give you access to the rest of it (section 59 (Severability) of PAIA).

### 11.6 If your request is refused

11.6.1 Tsamaya has no internal appeal. You may nevertheless ask our Information Officer to reconsider a decision; doing so is voluntary and does not extend the time for a complaint. You may complain to the Information Regulator within 180 days of our decision (sections 77A and 77B of PAIA), using Form 5 prescribed by the PAIA Regulations, 2021, or apply to a court (section 78 of PAIA).
