04 · How it works

What happens when you press Go.

  1. Step 01

    Set a destination

    Search, tap the map, or press and hold to drop a pin.

  2. Step 02

    Test the quickest route

    Tsamaya takes the quickest route and tests it against every rated area, using the ratings for this hour.

  3. Step 03

    Bend it onto checked roads

    If the route runs through a high-risk area, it moves it onto roads we have checked, and tells you what it went around.

  4. Step 04

    Keep the detour sensible

    A detour only gets offered if it cuts your exposure. One that adds too much distance is thrown out, even when it carries less risk.

  5. Step 05

    Say so when nothing is better

    When there is no good alternative, Tsamaya says so and gives you the normal route with the risky stretches marked. It will not invent a detour to look busy.

  6. Step 06

    Drive it your way

    Turn-by-turn in the app with voice, on CarPlay and Android Auto, or hand it to Google Maps with the detour points already in place.

06 · Questions

Straight answers.

Does it guarantee I will be safe?

No, and we will never tell you otherwise. It cuts your exposure to areas with a history of vehicle crime. Crime is not predictable. Treat it as better information for a choice you were going to make anyway, and stay alert.

Is it free?

Yes. No ads, and we do not sell anything about you.

Does a lower-risk route take much longer?

Usually a few minutes. Anything dramatically longer than the direct route is rejected.

What happens outside the 12 metros?

It works as a normal map and navigator, without risk data.

Can I switch to night ratings myself?

No. The app reads the clock: daytime ratings from 05:00 to 17:30, evening from 17:30 to 19:30 and night from 19:30 to 05:00. There is no manual switch to forget.

Johannesburg

Where Tsamaya started, and still the busiest part of the map.

Red areas in Johannesburg

315

areas rated high risk in Johannesburg, daytime ratings, 05:00 to 17:30

Johannesburg is where Tsamaya started and it is still the busiest part of the map. Almost everything funnels through a handful of roads: the M1 and M2, Empire, Louis Botha, Oxford. Those roads run close to the places where vehicle crime concentrates. Plan purely for speed and you will be sent through the worst of it at the worst time of night, without being told.

Driving here

Three things to know.

  1. The M1 and M2 carry the most flags

    The M1 and M2 through the inner city carry more flagged stretches than any other road we map. Most of it is smash-and-grab and robbery at off-ramps and traffic lights.

  2. Day and night differ sharply

    Ratings move sharply between day and night here. Several roads that carry no penalty at midday sit in the top band after 19:30.

  3. Load-shedding matters

    A dark intersection is a different proposition from a lit one, which is why evening and night are rated separately.

Johannesburg questions

Questions about Joburg.

Does it cover Soweto and the southern suburbs?

Yes. Coverage spans the whole municipal area.

Will it route me the long way round?

Only within limits. Most lower-risk routes across Johannesburg add a few minutes.

Does it work on the highways out of Joburg?

Yes. The N1, N3, N12 and N17 are mapped, and the major freeways carry protective ratings, so the app will not push you off a freeway onto side roads to shave a score.
