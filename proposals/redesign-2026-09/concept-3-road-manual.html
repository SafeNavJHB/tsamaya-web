Get the app

Read the road before you drive it.

Tsamaya is a free navigation app for South African drivers. It checks your route against 4 396 rated areas in 12 metros, at the hour you are driving, and steers you around the high-risk ones.

Tsamaya (say: tsa-MAH-ya): Sesotho and Setswana for "go".

Three ratings a day

Risk moves with the light. Every rated area carries a daytime, an evening and a night rating, and Tsamaya plans with the one for the hour you drive. The app reads the clock; there is no switch to forget.

How it works

The same six steps run every time you press Go.

  1. 1

    Set a destination

    Search, tap the map, or press and hold to drop a pin.

  2. 2

    It tests the quickest route

    Tsamaya checks it against every rated area, using the ratings for this hour.

  3. 3

    It moves you onto checked roads

    If the route runs through a high-risk area, it shifts it onto roads we have checked and tells you what it went around.

  4. 4

    A detour has to earn its place

    One is offered only if it cuts your exposure. A detour that adds too much distance is thrown out, even when it carries less risk.

  5. 5

    No detour for show

    With no good alternative, it says so and gives you the normal route with the risky stretches marked.

  6. 6

    Drive it

    Turn-by-turn with voice, CarPlay and Android Auto. Or hand it to Google Maps with the detour points already in place.

A few minutes, usually

On most trips the lower-risk route costs a few minutes. There is a ceiling: a detour that adds too much distance is thrown out, even when it carries less risk.

Sometimes there is no good alternative. Then Tsamaya tells you so, gives you the normal route and marks the risky stretches. It will not invent a detour to look busy.

Where it works

Twelve metros so far. The ratings come from published South African crime statistics, scored against OpenStreetMap road data to find where vehicle crime concentrates. Every metro gets a review pass before it goes live, and drivers correct it from there.

On the road

Press Start and Tsamaya becomes a full navigator, with the ratings running underneath.

  • Voice turn-by-turn

    Every turn called out ahead of time, with the road name.

  • CarPlay and Android Auto

    The same route and ratings on your car's screen.

  • Police and roadblock notices

    Reported by other drivers, shown for an hour and spoken when they are ahead of you. They never change your route.

  • Road closures

    Closures and protest reports are picked up daily and routed around.

  • Share a live trip

    Someone at home follows your drive on the website.

  • Hand it to Google Maps

    Prefer Google? The detour points go across with the route.

Questions

Four short answers.

Is it free?

Yes. No ads, and we do not sell anything about you.

Does a lower-risk route take much longer?

Usually a few minutes. Anything dramatically longer than the direct route is rejected.

Does it guarantee I will be safe?

No, and we will never tell you otherwise. It cuts your exposure to areas with a history of vehicle crime. Crime is not predictable.

Treat it as better information for a choice you were going to make anyway, and stay alert.

What happens outside the 12 metros?

It works as a normal map and navigator, without risk data.

Gauteng · 800 rated areas

Johannesburg is where Tsamaya started and it is still the busiest part of the map. Almost everything funnels through a handful of roads: the M1 and M2, Empire, Louis Botha, Oxford. Those roads run close to the places where vehicle crime concentrates. Plan purely for speed and you will be sent through the worst of it at the worst time of night, without being told.

Johannesburg questions

Does it cover Soweto and the southern suburbs?

Yes. Coverage spans the whole municipal area.

Will it route me the long way round?

Only within limits. Most lower-risk routes across Johannesburg add a few minutes.

Does it work on the highways out of Joburg?

Yes. The N1, N3, N12 and N17 are mapped, and the major freeways carry protective ratings, so the app will not push you off a freeway onto side roads to shave a score.
