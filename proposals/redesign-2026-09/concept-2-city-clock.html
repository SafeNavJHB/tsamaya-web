05:00Before the day starts · daytime ratings

A map that checks what your route drives through

Most maps work out the quickest way there. Tsamaya also works out what you would be driving through, and steers you around the areas where vehicle crime happens.

It is free, and in open beta on Android and iPhone. There are ratings for 12 metros, from Cape Town and Mossel Bay to Durban, Johannesburg and Pilanesberg. Outside them it still works as an ordinary map and navigator.

Where the name comes from

Tsamaya is Sesotho and Setswana for "go". It comes from "tsamaya sentle", go well, which is what you say to someone as they leave. In kasi football it is also the move that sends the defender the wrong way.

07:30The commute · daytime ratings

How Tsamaya plans a drive

  1. Pick where you are going

    Search, tap the map, or press and hold to drop a pin.

  2. It tests the quickest route

    Tsamaya takes the quickest route and checks it against every rated area, using the ratings for this hour.

  3. It moves you off the worst of it

    If the route runs through a high-risk area, Tsamaya shifts it onto roads we have checked, and tells you what it went around.

  4. The detour has to earn its place

    It is only offered if it cuts your exposure. A detour that adds too much distance is thrown out, even when it carries less risk.

  5. No detour for show

    When there is no good alternative, Tsamaya says so and gives you the normal route with the risky stretches marked.

  6. Drive it

    Turn-by-turn with voice, on CarPlay or Android Auto. Or hand it to Google Maps with the detour points already in place.

12:00The trade-off · daytime ratings

It usually costs a few minutes.

A detour is only offered if it cuts your exposure. If it adds too much distance it gets thrown out, even when it carries less risk. Sometimes the cost is nothing at all: on the route card above, both options take 25 minutes.

When there is no better way, Tsamaya tells you so and marks the risky stretches on the normal route. It will not invent a detour to look busy.

17:30Sun going down · evening ratings

At 17:30 the evening ratings take over.

1 524

areas are rated high risk across 12 metros between 17:30 and 19:30. That is 152 more than in the daytime.

Evening gets its own band because the light changes the road. Load-shedding makes it sharper: a dark intersection is a different proposition from a lit one, which is why evening and night are rated separately.

Tsamaya reads your phone's clock and switches on its own. There is no setting to remember.

22:00Questions · night ratings

What people ask before they install it

Is it free?

Yes. No ads, and we do not sell anything about you.

Does a lower-risk route take much longer?

Usually a few minutes. Anything dramatically longer than the direct route is rejected.

Does it guarantee I will be safe?

No, and we will never tell you otherwise. It cuts your exposure to areas with a history of vehicle crime. Crime is not predictable. Treat it as better information for a choice you were going to make anyway, and stay alert. Lower risk is not no risk.

What happens outside the 12 metros?

It works as a normal map and navigator, without risk data.

Johannesburg is where Tsamaya started and it is still the busiest part of the map. Almost everything funnels through a handful of roads: the M1 and M2, Empire, Louis Botha, Oxford. Those roads run close to the places where vehicle crime concentrates. Plan purely for speed and you will be sent through the worst of it at the worst time of night, without being told.

Several roads that carry no penalty at midday sit in the top band after 19:30.

Ratings move sharply between day and night here. That is the whole reason Tsamaya reads the clock.

The M1 and M2

Through the inner city they carry more flagged stretches than any other road we map. Most of it is smash-and-grab and robbery at off-ramps and traffic lights.

Johannesburg questions

Does it cover Soweto and the southern suburbs?

Yes. Coverage spans the whole municipal area.

Will it route me the long way round?

Only within limits. Most lower-risk routes across Johannesburg add a few minutes.

Does it work on the highways out of Joburg?

Yes. The N1, N3, N12 and N17 are mapped, and the major freeways carry protective ratings, so the app will not push you off a freeway onto side roads to shave a score.
